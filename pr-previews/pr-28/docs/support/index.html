Support for Eclipse Enclave

Eclipse Enclave is developed as an open source project. If your team is building on Enclave and needs help beyond the community channels, professional support and services are available from contributing companies. You can also sponsor the project directly to fund ongoing development.

Professional support and services​

The following contributing companies offer professional support, adoption guidance, and custom development for Eclipse Enclave.

eclipsesource.com

Long-time contributor to the Eclipse open source ecosystem and principal developer of Eclipse Enclave. EclipseSource helps organizations adopt, extend, and operate Enclave across their AI coding platform, from first pilot to production rollout.

  • Adoption and integration support for teams rolling out Enclave
  • Custom tool profiles and feature extensions for internal or third-party agents
  • Security hardening and network policy design for regulated environments
  • Training for developers, platform teams, and security reviewers
  • Sponsored development of new features and integrations
Get in touch with EclipseSource→

Project sponsoring​

Sponsoring keeps the project healthy: it funds core maintenance, security updates, and new capabilities that benefit every user of Enclave.

Sponsored development

Directly fund features, integrations, or hardening work that your organization needs. You shape the roadmap for the areas you invest in while everyone benefits from the resulting improvements.

Sustaining sponsorship

Support the project's long-term maintenance without steering individual features. Your contribution funds ongoing upkeep: security patches, dependency updates, agent compatibility, and general project health.

If you are interested in either model, contact one of the professional support providers above to discuss the arrangement that fits your organization.

Community support​

For everyday questions, bug reports, and feature ideas, the project's GitHub repository is the place to go. Maintainers and community members follow it and respond there.

  • Report an issue or request a feature
  • Browse and join discussions

Please keep in mind that community channels rely on volunteer time. If you need guaranteed response times, priority fixes, or bespoke work, professional support is the right fit.
